reKing (PPSA99063): The Return of the King (US Xbox) statically recompiled for the PS5

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts, New Line Cinema, Warner Bros.,
the Tolkien Estate, Microsoft or Sony Interactive Entertainment. It holds nothing from the game: the game's code is
recompiled from the player's own default.xbe, and the game's files are read from the player's own disc image.

What is in the app, and under which terms

- reKing's own code, the PS5 host (ps5/: app shell, entry, input, audio, guest memory) and its changes to the SDK
  and the port: GPL-3.0 (reKing-GPL-3.0.txt). Parts follow reUndr's PS5 host by bystritskiy (MIT, reUndr-MIT.txt).
- The Return of the King Xbox port from Athelas by Shagg (MIT, Athelas-MIT.txt).
- XboxXBE-Recomp by TGP482, the recompiler and runtime (MIT, XboxXBE-Recomp-MIT.txt).
- Dear ImGui (MIT), toml++ (MIT), stb (public domain / MIT), the Khronos Vulkan headers (Apache-2.0 / MIT),
  and glslang 15.4.0 (BSD / MIT / Apache-2.0, glslang.txt).
- RADV from Mesa (MIT and others, Mesa-license.rst), built with PS5_Vulkan's recipe, which with its native link
  recipe and the PS5 Platform library from the PS5_PayloadSDK fork is GPL-3.0-or-later (PS5_Vulkan-LICENSE.txt,
  PS5_PayloadSDK-LICENSE.txt). The combined executable is therefore subject to the GPL-3.0-or-later.

The game, its assets and the code generated from it belong to their owners and are not covered by any licence here.
